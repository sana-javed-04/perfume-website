HOW PERFUME WORKS

The Three Scent Stages

A good perfume changes gently over time on your skin. Here is how our scents unfold throughout the day:

01

First Smell (0 to 30 mins)

Fresh bergamot citrus and soft spices you smell right after spraying.

02

Middle Scent (2 to 7 hours)

The main body of fresh roses, jasmine, and warm lavender flowers.

03

Base Note (8 to 18 hours)

Deep amber, sandalwood, and vanilla that stay on your clothes all day.
